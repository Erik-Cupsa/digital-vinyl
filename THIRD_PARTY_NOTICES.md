## Inspiration, not copied implementations

[SPNFY by Saqoosha](https://github.com/Saqoosha/SPNFY) (MIT, copyright 2025 Saqoosha)
inspired the album-art-on-record idea. It targets different hardware and a different
service; its code and artwork are not included.

[spotify-deck-esp32 by jayesh-cmd](https://github.com/jayesh-cmd/spotify-deck-esp32)
was consulted as a host-to-display reference. At the time of review it had no
declared repository license; no source was copied. Its Mac browser automation
approach and 160 × 128 ST7735 target are not used here.

Album covers retrieved at runtime belong to their respective rights holders.
No commercial album art, listening history, credentials, device flash dumps or
personal Chrome data are bundled. Demo covers are generated by original code.
